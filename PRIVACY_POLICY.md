# Privacy Policy for TRIminder

**Last Updated**: December 2024

## Introduction

TRIminder ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our digital wellness application.

## Information We Collect

### Screen Time Data
- **Screen ON/OFF events**: We track when your device screen turns on and off
- **Session duration**: Time spent with screen active
- **Idle detection**: Whether you were actively using the device
- **Daily totals**: Aggregated screen time per day

### User Account Information
- **Email address**: For authentication and account management
- **Full name**: For personalization and social features
- **Department**: For organizational rankings (if applicable)
- **User tag**: Public identifier for social features

### Technical Information
- **Device information**: Android version, manufacturer
- **App usage**: Feature usage within TRIminder
- **Performance data**: App crashes and performance metrics

## How We Use Your Information

### Primary Purposes
- **Screen Time Tracking**: Monitor and record your device usage patterns
- **Gamification**: Calculate XP, levels, and achievements based on screen time
- **Social Features**: Enable friend connections and rankings
- **Data Synchronization**: Sync data across your devices

### Analytics and Improvement
- **App Performance**: Monitor app stability and performance
- **Feature Usage**: Understand which features are most valuable
- **User Experience**: Improve app design and functionality

## Data Storage and Security

### Local Storage
- **SQLite Database**: All screen time data is stored locally on your device
- **Encryption**: Local data is encrypted using device security features
- **Offline Capability**: App works without internet connection

### Cloud Storage (Optional)
- **Supabase Backend**: Encrypted cloud storage for data synchronization
- **Authentication**: Secure user authentication and session management
- **Data Sync**: Optional synchronization across multiple devices

### Security Measures
- **End-to-End Encryption**: All cloud communications are encrypted
- **Secure Authentication**: Industry-standard authentication protocols
- **Data Minimization**: We only collect data necessary for app functionality

## Data Sharing and Disclosure

### We Do NOT Share
- **Screen time data** with third parties
- **Personal information** with advertisers
- **Usage patterns** with marketing companies
- **Location data** (we don't collect it)

### We May Share
- **Aggregated, anonymized data** for research purposes (no personal identifiers)
- **Technical information** with service providers (Supabase, Flutter)
- **Legal compliance** when required by law

## Your Rights and Choices

### Data Control
- **Export Data**: Request a copy of your data
- **Delete Account**: Remove all your data from our systems
- **Opt-out**: Disable cloud synchronization
- **Local-only Mode**: Use app without cloud features

### Privacy Settings
- **Screen Time Tracking**: Can be disabled (app functionality will be limited)
- **Social Features**: Can be disabled to prevent data sharing
- **Cloud Sync**: Can be disabled for local-only usage
- **Notifications**: Can be customized or disabled

## Permissions and Justification

### Required Permissions
- **Battery Optimization Bypass**: Required for 24/7 background tracking
- **Internet Access**: For authentication and optional cloud sync
- **Wake Lock**: Prevents device sleep during active tracking

### Optional Permissions
- **Notifications** (Android 13+): For foreground service notifications
- **Exact Alarm** (Android 12+): For reliable timer functionality

### Permission Usage
- **Battery Optimization**: Maintains background service for continuous tracking
- **Internet**: User authentication and data synchronization
- **Wake Lock**: Ensures accurate session timing

## Data Retention

### Local Data
- **Screen time logs**: Retained indefinitely on your device
- **User preferences**: Stored locally until app uninstall
- **Cache data**: Automatically cleaned periodically

### Cloud Data
- **Account information**: Retained while account is active
- **Screen time data**: Synced data retained for 2 years
- **Deleted accounts**: Data purged within 30 days
